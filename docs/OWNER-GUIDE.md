# Your APB Detailing website

## Log in

Open your website address with `/admin` at the end. Use the email and password set up for you. There is no public admin link. Keep your password private and log out on shared devices.

If you forget it, try **Forgot your password?** If no email arrives, ask your site maintainer to reset it. The free setup does not depend on buying an email service.

## Add a car

1. Open **Manage showcase** and choose **Add car**.
2. Enter the make/model, optional year, work description and services performed.
3. Choose **Save car & add photos**. It is a private draft.
4. Choose **Upload photos** and select photos from your phone. Wait for processing and uploading to finish.
5. Pick a cover photograph. Add useful descriptions and use the arrows to change the order.
6. Check the customer is happy for you to share the photos. Avoid showing personal information unnecessarily.
7. Choose **Publish vehicle**. Use **View showcase** to check it.

The featured switch puts a car ahead of other entries on the homepage. Lower display-order numbers come first within each group.

## Photographs

Each car has a **maximum of 10 photographs**, including the cover. The counter shows how many slots you have used.

Your phone photos are resized and compressed before upload. The originals on your phone stay unchanged. The largest edge will be no more than 1920 pixels and each uploaded file no more than about 500 KB. Very detailed images may be reduced a little further to fit.

Use clear photos of the overall car, paintwork, wheels and interior. You don't need all 10 slots. If a photo cannot be processed, it is not uploaded. HEIC support varies by phone/browser; export a JPEG copy from Photos if needed. Extremely large files over 60 MB need a smaller exported copy first.

An incomplete upload may leave a reserved slot. It will say **Upload incomplete**. Delete it to free the slot, then try again. If deletion fails because the connection is down, retry when it returns.

For before-and-after comparisons, choose **Before** on one photo and **After** on another. They use two of the 10 slots. Use matching angles and framing for the best comparison. Only one before and one after photo can be selected; clear an existing role before assigning it elsewhere.

Delete a photo to replace it. If you delete the last finished photo, the vehicle becomes a draft automatically. Deleting a car removes its photos too. Permanent deletion asks you to confirm first.

## Edit services and prices

**Manage services** lets you change names, descriptions, photos, order and visibility. Choose **Save service** after editing. Lower order numbers appear first.

**Manage pricing** supports price on request, a fixed price or a starting price. Enter any short pricing note and save. To show a price, enable it for that service **and** turn on **Show prices publicly**. Keep that main switch off until the price list is confirmed.

## Read enquiries

Open **Contact enquiries** regularly. There are no automatic email notifications in this free setup. Each enquiry shows the customer's details, requested service and message.

Change its status from **New** to **Contacted**, then **Closed** as appropriate. Email and telephone links open your usual apps. Delete old enquiries when you no longer need the information; deletion is permanent.

## Update contact details

Open **Business settings**, make your changes and save. Leave unknown details blank.

- Enter phone and WhatsApp numbers in international format, beginning `+44` for UK numbers. Remove the first `0` from the local number after `+44`.
- A WhatsApp button appears only when a valid number is saved.
- The address stays private until **Publish this address** is ticked.
- Add confirmed opening hours and social links when ready.

## If the site cannot load your work

The homepage may still work while the portfolio or login is unavailable. Ask the maintainer to check whether the free Supabase project has paused and restore it. Do not keep retrying large uploads. Keep original photos and backups so your work can be restored if needed.
